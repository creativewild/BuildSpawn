Settings overview

The Manage page lists every settings page of the server. This page tells you what each one is and where it is described in this guide.

When to use it: when you know what you want to change but not which page to open, or when you want to see everything an administrator can set.

How to open the Settings pages

  1. Click Manage in the left rail.
  2. Find the page in the list. You only see the pages your permissions allow.
  3. Click it. Use the breadcrumb to come back to Manage.

The pages that appear depend on the plugins that are installed and on your permissions. A person who only has the System read permission sees many pages read-only.

Tips

  • Give the Manage permission to very few people. It lets a person change the settings of the whole server.
  • The Script Console and Reload Configuration pages are the most powerful. Use them with care.
  • After you change a setting, check the page again. A field that shows a red message was not saved.

Project settings

Every setting of a project, what it holds and where to find it.

The project settings page

Open Settings on a project. It has sub-tabs. General has the name, description, documentation, icon, views, order of items and health metrics. The properties below are on the full form. ID, Versioned Settings and Connections follow. Build settings and Templates pass steps, parameters and templates down. Perforce roots and Credentials are shared with the build configurations below.

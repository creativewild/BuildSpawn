Retention and discard old builds

Rules that delete old builds and their files after each build, so the disk does not fill up.

When to use it

Use it when builds and artifacts take much space. A build of a game can have gigabytes of artifacts. Keep the last 30 builds, and keep the artifacts of fewer. The rules run after every build, not at once when you save. The card is on General under Clean-up rules.

How to set it up

  1. Open the build configuration and click Settings. Then click General.
  2. Go to Clean-up rules and turn on Discard old builds.
  3. Choose the Strategy: Keep by age and number.
  4. Set Days to keep builds or Builds to keep, or both.
  5. Set Days to keep artifacts or Builds to keep artifacts of to remove artifacts sooner than the builds.
  6. Click Save.

Every field

Keep rules are whole numbers from 1. An empty field means no limit. The card shows a line In words that says what the rules do.

Builds that you pin with Keep this build forever, and the last stable and last successful build, are never deleted by these rules. If both a number and an age are set, a build is deleted when either limit is passed.

A worked example

The studio keeps the last 30 builds of Windows Build and the artifacts of the last 5. The game package of 2 GB is then kept for 5 builds only. The log and the change list stay for 30. See Keep the last 30 builds.

What you see when it runs

After each build, builds and artifacts outside the rules disappear from the History list. The summary says Nothing is deleted when no limit is set. An administrator can also set a server-wide rule in the global settings.

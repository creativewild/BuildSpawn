Shared builds

Share a personal build with a link, so a colleague can look at its result and log without getting any other access.

When to use it

A personal build is private: only you and administrators see it. Share it when someone else should read its log, for example a programmer who helps you with a failing shelf, or a lead who wants to see a build before you submit.

Share a build

  1. Open the personal build. The Share button is in the header, next to the other build actions. Only the person who started the build, and administrators, see it.
  2. Click Share. Buildspawn makes a link and copies it. A message says Link copied. If your browser refuses to copy, the link is shown in a box so you can copy it by hand.
  3. Send the link to your colleague.

The link ends in ?share= and a long random code. Clicking Share again gives the same link.

What the other person sees

  • They must be signed in and may read Buildspawn. The link then lets them view that one build, and only for their browser session.
  • They can read the build page, console, changes, parameters and artifacts.
  • They do not see Re-run, and they do not see your other personal builds.

Stop sharing

  1. Open the build and click Stop sharing. The button appears while the build is shared.
  2. A message says Sharing stopped. The link no longer works. The link and every session that used it lose access at once.

Problems and fixes

ProblemWhat to do
There is no Share button.The build is not personal, or you did not start it. Only personal builds can be shared.
The colleague gets page not found.They may not be signed in, or you stopped sharing. Share again and send the new link.
The browser could not copy the link.Select the link in the box that opens and copy it yourself.

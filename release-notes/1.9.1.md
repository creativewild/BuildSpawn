# Buildspawn 1.9.1 (build 2.589-buildspawn.19)

## Fixes

**Statistics tab:**
- The charts fit inside their cards at every desktop size, Windows scaling and sidebar width. They were about 12 px too wide everywhere.
- The charts redraw when the page gets narrower: when its scrollbar appears, or when you resize or fold the sidebar.
- The axis labels are readable. Before, they had a light outline that made them look washed out.
- For short ranges the date axis shows times, and it never repeats the same label.
